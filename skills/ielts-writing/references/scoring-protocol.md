# Task 2 scoring protocol

## 1. Establish assessability

Confirm that the task is IELTS Writing Task 2 and that the response contains assessable English. Diagnostics such as word and paragraph counts are audit context only.

## 2. Make four independent decisions

For each criterion separately:

1. Read the relevant official-reference summaries across the whole scale.
2. Identify the higher-band features sustained across the response before cataloguing limitations.
3. Identify the best-fitting current band from the performance demonstrated throughout the response.
4. Test the adjacent lower and higher bands rather than starting from an Overall impression.
5. Record current performance, separate exact positive and limitation evidence, why the adjacent lower band is ruled out, and why the next band is not yet fully demonstrated.
6. Return one whole-number criterion band from 0 to 9.

Weakness in one criterion must not cap another. Do not choose an Overall level first, force criteria into a narrow vector, or lower a score merely because evidence is mixed. Mixed evidence is resolved by the descriptor that best represents the sustained performance and limitations.

Evaluate errors by their frequency, range, effect on readability, and the proportion of error-free sentences. A single local error cannot set the band. Do not count a spelling error in both LR and GRA, use Band 9 perfection as the test for Band 7, or infer a lower score from the teaching advice you would give. Sustained Band 7 or Band 8 features retain their descriptor band despite isolated imperfections.

Occasional omissions or lapses remain compatible with Band 8 in Task Response, Coherence and Cohesion, and Lexical Resource. A few errors that do not impair communication remain compatible with Band 8 in Grammatical Range and Accuracy. Band 6 requires limitations characteristic of Band 6 across the response; the mere existence of an imperfection is not enough. Any claim that a limitation is recurring or pervasive must be supported by multiple exact examples from different locations.

## 3. Evidence rules

Every evidence item must be a short, contiguous, exact, locatable quotation from the submitted response. Keep positive and limitation evidence separate. Do not join distant fragments, add ellipses, invent, silently correct, or paraphrase evidence.

## 4. Uncertainty

Mark uncertainty as `low` when the evidence supports one clear whole-band decision. Mark it as `material` only when limited or genuinely conflicting evidence leaves an adjacent-band decision plausible. State whether the plausible adjacent direction is lower or higher and explain why. Uncertainty is not a reason to apply a systematic downward adjustment.

## 5. Lock before teaching

Validate the four decisions and calculate Overall in application code. After this point the scores are locked. Teaching output has three distinct responsibilities:

- Current performance: restate what the response presently demonstrates.
- Why not the next band: explain the descriptor gap using the locked evidence.
- Next training action: give a focused exercise that could close that gap.

Teaching advice may recommend any construction or strategy that fits the student's actual need, but it must not present a preferred sentence type, template, vocabulary count, or stylistic choice as a scoring requirement.
